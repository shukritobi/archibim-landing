03 / Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need both plugins?

No. Joinery can run independently. Core adds supported wall-binding and synchronization features.

Which SketchUp version is supported?

SketchUp Desktop 2026 is the primary development and testing target. These Ruby extensions are not available in SketchUp Web or iPad.

When will ArchiBIM be available?

Both extensions are in active development and desktop testing. Register your interest to hear about availability, demos and early-access opportunities.

Is Joinery a fabrication-ready system?

It supports panel-based modeling and preliminary cutlists. Manufacturing dimensions, hardware and structural details must be verified separately.
